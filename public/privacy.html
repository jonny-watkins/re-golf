Deleting your account

You can delete your account from "My account" when you have no order in progress. We then remove your display name, listings, photographs, saved clubs and delivery addresses, remove your email address from our sign-in system, and close your Stripe payout account. We keep records of your completed orders because the law requires it. Messages you sent stay visible to the person you sent them to, shown as from a deleted member.
