6. How payments are held and released

  • We hold the buyer's payment through Stripe until the order is complete. The seller is not paid at the time of purchase.
  • If the seller does not post the item within 5 days, the order is cancelled and the buyer is refunded in full.
  • After the buyer confirms delivery there is a 48-hour checking period. If the buyer does not confirm delivery, the item is treated as delivered 10 days after posting.
  • If the buyer reports a problem during the checking period, payment is paused. The seller may refund the buyer, or require the item to be returned first to an address the seller gives. The buyer must then post it within 5 days using a tracked service and enter the tracking number; if they do not, the problem is closed and the seller is paid. The buyer is refunded when the seller confirms the item has been returned, or automatically 10 days after it was posted back unless the seller has asked us to decide. The buyer may withdraw the problem, in which case the seller is paid.
  • Either party may ask us to decide a reported problem, and we decide any order involving a card dispute. We may ask both parties for information and may read the messages about the order. We will decide, acting reasonably, whether to release the payment to the seller or refund the buyer. Our decision on the held payment is final as between you and us; it does not affect any other legal rights you have against the other party.
  • If no problem is reported, the payment (item price plus delivery charge) is released to the seller at the end of the checking period. The buyer may release it earlier.
  • Refunds are made in full to the original payment method, including the fee and delivery charge. Where an item is returned, the buyer pays the return postage. [Confirm this position with the adviser, including where the item was not as described.]
