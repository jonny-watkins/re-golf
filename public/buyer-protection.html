How buying and selling works

Relofted is a marketplace where golfers buy and sell second-hand golf equipment from each other. Every payment goes through Relofted and is held until the buyer has the item and has had time to check it.

For buyers

  1. You see the full price before you pay. The total is the item price, the buyer protection fee and tracked delivery. Nothing is added later.
  2. You pay by card through our payment provider, Stripe. Relofted never sees or stores your card details.
  3. We hold your payment. The seller is not paid when you buy.
  4. The seller must post within 5 days, using a tracked service. If they do not, the order is cancelled and you are refunded in full automatically.
  5. When the item arrives, tell us on your Orders screen. You then have 48 hours to check it.
  6. If something is wrong, report it within those 48 hours. Payment to the seller is paused straight away. The seller can refund you in full, or ask for the item back first.
  7. If the seller asks for the item back, you have 5 days to post it to the address they give, using a tracked service, and enter the tracking number. You pay the return postage. You are refunded in full, including the fee and delivery, when the seller confirms it has arrived, or automatically 10 days after you posted it if they do not respond. If you do not post it back in time, the problem is closed and the seller is paid.
  8. If you and the seller cannot agree, either of you can ask our support team to decide. If we agree the item was not as described, or it never arrived, you are refunded in full.
  9. If everything is fine, the seller is paid when the 48 hours end. You can release the payment sooner if you are happy.

If you do not confirm delivery, the item is treated as delivered 10 days after it was posted, and the 48-hour checking period starts then.

The buyer protection fee

Buyers pay a fee of 70p plus 5% of the item price on every purchase. It pays for holding your money safely, card processing and support if something goes wrong. Prices on listings are shown with and without this fee.

For sellers

  1. Listing is free and there is no selling fee. You receive the full item price plus the delivery charge the buyer paid.
  2. When your item sells, we show you the buyer's delivery address. Post it within 5 days using a tracked service, and enter the carrier and tracking number on your Orders screen. Keep your proof of posting.
  3. You are paid after delivery. Your money is released 48 hours after the buyer confirms delivery (or sooner if they release it), unless they report a problem.
  4. If the buyer reports a problem, you choose: refund them, or ask for the item back first at an address you give. You are not asked to refund until the item has been posted back with tracking. If it does not arrive, or is not what you sent, ask our support team to decide before the refund is made.
  5. Set up payouts once. To receive money you complete a short identity and bank details form with Stripe. Your earnings wait safely until you do.

Staying protected

  • Always pay through the Relofted checkout. If someone asks you to pay by bank transfer or outside the site, do not, and report the conversation.
  • Keep your conversation with the other person in Relofted messages. Our support team can read the messages about an order when a problem is reported.
  • Describe items honestly, including wear and any damage, and photograph the actual item.
